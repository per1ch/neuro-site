# Stop the bleeding first

When someone is hurt, **bleeding kills fastest**. Check the limbs for [Arterial Bleeding](#/affliction/arterialcut) and apply a [Tourniquet](#/item/tourniquet).

> [!WARNING]
> A tourniquet left on too long causes gangrene.

## Then scan

1. Grab a [Health Scanner](#/item/healthscanner).
2. Look for hidden problems like [Pneumothorax](#/affliction/pneumothorax).
3. Treat the most dangerous thing first.

> [!TIP]
> Use the symptom checker if you're unsure what you're looking at.
